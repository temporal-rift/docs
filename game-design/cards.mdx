title: "Cards"
description: "Card pool, types, and categories"
---


All players share the same card pool. Each era you are dealt **7 cards** and choose exactly **5** during a private
hand-selection window before Action Round 1, then play one per action round. Hands do not carry over — unplayed cards
are discarded at the end of the era. Each dealt card slot selects a category, then a card within that category, then a
grade; this keeps the deal mix stable as card types are added.

The hand-selection duration is configuration, not a fixed game constant: its starting values are 60 seconds for a
3-player game, 45 seconds for 4 players, and 30 seconds for 5 players. If you miss the window, the system chooses a
five-card subset uniformly at random from the seven dealt. You lose the ability to filter cards, but the default does
not optimize or systematically bias the resulting hand by category, grade, or deal order.

## Probability Shifters

| Card | Effect | `cardType` |
|---|---|---|
| **Push** | +20% to one outcome | `PUSH` |
| **Suppress** | −20% to one outcome | `SUPPRESS` |
| **Swing** | Move 30% from one outcome to another on same event | `SWING` |
| **Amplify** | Double a named player's card this round | `AMPLIFY` |

## Information

| Card | Effect | `cardType` |
|---|---|---|
| **Intercept** | See a random card from a named player's hand secretly | `INTERCEPT` |
| **Scan** | See live exact probabilities for one event for the rest of the era | `SCAN` |
| **Trace** | See which players influenced a specific event last round | `TRACE` |
| **Decoy** | Play a fake action visible to others but with no effect | `DECOY` |

Information cards reveal to you and nobody else. Intercept selects its revealed card uniformly at random from the target
hand; the player you trace or intercept is never told.

## Disruption

| Card | Effect | `cardType` |
|---|---|---|
| **Jam** | Named player cannot use faction specials next round | `JAM` |
| **Stall** | Defer one event's resolution to the next era (one resolution cycle) | `STALL` |
| **Redirect** | Move a named player's shift this round to a different outcome | `REDIRECT` |
| **Nullify** | Cancel a named player's action this round | `NULLIFY` |

Nullify, Redirect, Amplify, Jam and Intercept all name a **player** as their target. Every other card targets an event.
A jammed player can still play cards — only faction specials are blocked, and only for one round.

## Paradox

| Card | Effect | `cardType` |
|---|---|---|
| **Collide** | Force two outcomes to equal probability, guaranteeing a paradox | `COLLIDE` |
| **Stabilize** | Prevent a paradox from triggering on one event this round | `STABILIZE` |
| **Detonate** | If a paradox exists, double its negative score effect on all players except you | `DETONATE` |

## Card grades

Graded cards carry grade **I**, **II** or **III**, which varies their magnitude or scope. Grade II is the baseline.

| Card | I | II | III |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Push** | +10% | +20% | +30% |
| **Suppress** | −10% | −20% | −30% |
| **Swing** | 15% | 30% | 45% |
| **Amplify** | ×1.5 | ×2 | ×3 |
| **Scan** | 1 event | 2 events | all 3 |
| **Intercept** | 1 card | 2 cards | — |
| **Trace** | one event | all events | — |
| **Nullify** | cancel 1 | cancel 2 | — |

Jam, Stall, Decoy, Redirect and Collide are single-grade. Grade rarity is configurable; the starting distribution is
**I 60% · II 30% · III 10%**. High grades are rare, so holding one is a decision about when to spend it rather than a
free upgrade.

## When you can play a card

Some cards need something to have happened before them, or something still to come after. Rather than let you waste a
turn, those cards simply cannot be played in the round where they would do nothing — your client greys them out.

| Card | Cannot be played in | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Trace** | Era 1, Round 1 | no preceding round exists yet |
| **Jam** | Round 3 | no following round to suppress |
| **Scan** | Round 3 | no action left in the era to use the intel |
| **Intercept** | Round 3 | no action left in the era to use the intel |
| **Stabilize**, **Detonate** | Rounds 1–3 | reactive — Paradox Resolution only |

Trace reads across era boundaries: in Era 2 Round 1 it shows you Era 1 Round 3. Only the game's very first round has
no predecessor.

## Card categories

Cards are grouped by category in the domain model. The `CardType` enum carries a `getCategory()` method, and `CardType.byCategory(CardCategory)` returns the full set of types in a category.

```
PROBABILITY_SHIFTER  →  PUSH, SUPPRESS, SWING, AMPLIFY
INFORMATION          →  INTERCEPT, SCAN, TRACE, DECOY
DISRUPTION           →  JAM, STALL, REDIRECT, NULLIFY
PARADOX              →  COLLIDE, STABILIZE, DETONATE
```

Categories are also how hands are dealt: each card slot picks a category first, then a card within it, so the balance
of play styles in your hand stays consistent. The category weights are configuration, with these starting values:

| Category | Starting weight |
|---|---|
| Probability Shifter | 35% |
| Information | 25% |
| Disruption | 25% |
| Paradox | 15% |

Stabilize and Detonate are never in your action hand — they are dealt only if a Paradox Resolution phase opens.

The `RoundSummaryPublished` event exposes only category and family — never the specific card — to preserve information asymmetry for all players.
