title: "Era Structure"

---


Each era (round) follows a fixed sequence of phases.

## Phase sequence

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Reveal — 3 future events drawn] --> D[Declaration window — Rally or eligible Momentum]
  D --> B[Action Round 1 — Sealed, simultaneous]
  B --> C[Action Round 2 — Category & type of R1 visible]
  C --> D[Action Round 3 — Partial probability shifts from R2 visible]
  D --> E{Paradoxes?}
  E -->|None| G[Outcome — Final probs determine winners]
  E -->|Found| F[Paradox Resolution — All play one card]
  F --> G
  G --> H[Era End — Check win conditions]
  H -->|Winner| I[Game Over]
  H -->|No winner| A
```

## Phase descriptions

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| **Reveal** | 3 future events drawn and shown with their 3 possible outcomes |
| **Declaration window** | Before Round 1, an Activist may use Rally or eligible Momentum to publicly declare one current-era event/outcome; Momentum applies its one-time +10 percentage-point bonus here |
| **Action Round 1** | Players secretly submit 1 card or 1 special. Limited info — act on instinct |
| **Action Round 2** | Category and type of Round 1 actions visible. An Expose target's qualifying Round-1 signature is revealed at close. |
| **Action Round 3** | Partial probability shifts from Round 2 visible. A successful Expose announces only changed behaviour, not the response signature. |
| **Paradox Check** | System detects paradoxes. None: skip to Outcome. Found: open Paradox Resolution. |
| **Paradox Resolution** | All players play one card (no specials). Paradox resolves or cascades. |
| **Outcome** | Final probabilities determine winning outcomes. Scores updated per faction rules. |
| **Era End** | Check win conditions. Advance to next era or trigger game end. |

## Timers

Default timer: **60 seconds per action round** (configurable via `game.rules.action-round-timer-seconds`).

Timer is player-count-dependent by configuration — never hard-code it:

| Players | Timer |
|---|---|
| 3 | 60s |
| 4 | 45s |
| 5 | 30s |

## Information visibility by round

| Info revealed | After Round 1 | After Round 2 | After Round 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category + family of each action | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Probability band (LOW/MED/HIGH) | — | ✔ | ✔ |
| Exact probability (Scan only) | — | — | ✔ (to Scan player) |
| Which player played which | — | — | — (never public) |
| Activist declaration of record | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Exposed Round-1 signature | — | ✔ | ✔ |
| Expose Round-3 response signature | — | — | — (never public) |
