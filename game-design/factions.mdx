---
title: "Factions"
description: "Faction goals, win conditions, and special actions"
---


Each game uses exactly as many factions as there are players (3–5), drawn randomly from the roster of 5. Each player is
assigned exactly one faction secretly at game start. No two players share a faction.

## Faction goals and win conditions

| Faction             | Goal                                              | Win condition                                               |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| ⚡ **Erasers**       | Wipe outcomes completely                          | Annihilate 4+ leading outcomes OR reach 20 pts              |
| 📜 **Prophets**     | Events resolve as written                         | 4+ events resolve as written OR reach 20 pts                |
| 🔄 **Revisionists** | Secret preferred outcome changes each era         | Secret outcome wins 3+ eras OR reach 20 pts                 |
| 🧵 **Weavers**      | Build causal chains across eras                   | Complete a chain of 3+ connected events OR reach 20 pts     |
| 📣 **Activists**    | Publicly declare an outcome, score when it wins   | Correct declarations in 3+ consecutive eras OR reach 20 pts |

## Special actions

Each faction has 3 unique special actions, available regardless of what is in your hand. Each action round: play
**1 card OR 1 special — never both**.

Four specials are limited to **one use per era**, because repeating them every round would decide the game on its own:

| Special | Faction | Why it is limited |
|---|---|---|
| **Annihilate** | Erasers | used three times, it erases every outcome of an event |
| **Seal** | Prophets | repeated, it locks the whole board |
| **Corrupt** | Erasers | inverts an opponent's card, every round |
| **Mimic** | Revisionists | copies only transfers toward the named outcome, every round — never a card moving away from it |

The rest stay usable every round, subject to their own timing rules. This limit is also what makes your hand matter —
without it, a faction with a strong repeatable special would rarely need to play a card at all.

### ⚡ Erasers

| Special        | Effect                                                              |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Annihilate** | Remove one outcome from an event this era: it drops to 0% and its probability moves to the other outcomes. Rejected, at no cost, if the survivors can't hold it within 0–90% — so an event always keeps at least two outcomes in contention |
| **Corrupt**    | Invert the probability effect of another player's card this round   |
| **Cascade**    | If the erased outcome's event carries into next era (Stall or paradox cascade), erase it again there automatically so it can't be pushed back up; not playable in the final era |

### 📜 Prophets

| Special         | Effect                                                                       |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Foresight**   | See next era's fresh future events, with their printed starting weights, and write a current-era outcome; that writing expires without score if the event Stalls |
| **Seal**        | Lock one outcome's probability through the end of the era — later shifts against it are blocked with weights unchanged and no paradox; shifts that leave sealed weights unchanged reroute normally. A sealed outcome never absorbs an erased outcome's probability, and Annihilate can still erase it |
| **Fulfillment** | Declare a current-era event; score +8 if its written outcome wins this era. A Stall expires the declaration |

### 🔄 Revisionists

| Special     | Effect                                                                      |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Rewrite** | Swap secret preferred outcome to any available outcome                      |
| **Mimic**   | Independently reapply the strongest same-round inward transfer by another player toward the named outcome — a Push targeting it or a Swing destined for it (largest magnitude); a Suppress or a Swing away from it never qualifies, so naming an outcome always raises it or does nothing — a fresh shift at Mimic's own magnitude, not a copy of that card's already-applied result. A Trace of the named event shows the tracer your Mimic, which reveals you as the Revisionist |
| **Obscure** | Action Rounds 1–2 only. Disguise yourself for the next round: every Intercept on you samples one decoy hand the size of your real hand, holding only cards already revealed this era plus fresh decoys, and a Trace of that round leaves you out, so a Mimic you play that round cannot be traced |

### 🧵 Weavers

| Special      | Effect                                                                      |
|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Thread**   | Name a current-era, not-yet-resolved outcome as a pending link; it shifts in your favor, but a Stall clears the link without penalty |
| **Tapestry** | Arm protection for the chain's pending link for the rest of this era (requires chain of 2+); absorbs one Annihilate against it so the chain can't break, though the erased outcome can't win and the link clears — Thread again that era |
| **Reweave**  | Once per era, move the chain's pending link to a different unresolved current-era outcome; the link still has to win to count, so Reweave dodges an Annihilate on the predicted outcome but never adds length on its own |

### 📣 Activists

| Special      | Effect                                                                                   |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Rally**    | During the declaration window, publicly declare one current-era event/outcome; only Round-1 direct transfers into it use 150% base magnitude |
| **Expose**   | In Round 2 target a player publicly shown to have played a Probability Shifter card in Round 1. A live reveal of an actual Push, Suppress, or Swing signature at Round-2 close earns +2; a later changed response is informational. Amplify and a Decoy disguised as a Probability Shifter are accepted targets but reveal and score nothing. |
| **Momentum** | An Activist whose immediately preceding declaration succeeded may instead declare a new current-era target and apply one +10 percentage-point bonus |

An Activist has at most one Rally- or Momentum-mode declaration per era. The raw action remains private; its
declaration of record and Expose's approved disclosures are public. An Expose response signature is never revealed.
A stalled target makes the declaration fail in that era, breaks the consecutive-era streak, and does not grant
Momentum next era. The carried event may be declared again as a new bet.

A Round 1 Nullify naming the declaring Activist cancels that era's declaration entirely: no Rally boost, no Momentum
bonus, no declaration score even if the declared outcome wins, no streak credit, and no Momentum eligibility next era.
